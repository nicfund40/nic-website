About Us

The Nova Investment Club – Undergraduate Division was founded in 2013 with the objective of stimulating students' interest in, and understanding of, financial markets, and of bridging the gap between the academic and the corporate world.

Members manage the NIC-UD Fund, write the Quarterly Shareholders Report and present stock pitches. The club also organises office visits, an annual field trip to London, competitions and events, and produces the After the Bell podcast.

In 2026 NIC-UD hosted the first Portuguese Investment Clubs Gathering at Nova SBE, together with CLIP and TIC.
