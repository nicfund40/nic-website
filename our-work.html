Our Work

The Fund

The NIC-UD Fund was launched in February 2020. Its objective is to build a diversified portfolio of securities (equities, ETFs and bonds), to teach portfolio management to members, and to achieve the highest possible risk-adjusted return over the long term, outperforming its benchmark.

  • Long-only

    The fund does not short sell.

  • Sector-agnostic

    No sector focus; macro analysis sets over- and underweight targets.

  • Equity overweight

    Higher risk appetite given the investors' profile.

  • Value and growth

    A mix of both strategies across the portfolio.
